THINGS WE WONDERED ABOUT.

An ongoing investigation into computation as a way of thinking. Exploring programming, problem solving, abstraction, and the curious ways humans teach machines to reason (and sometimes teach ourselves in the process). The questions have outlived the projects that first inspired them. Some lines of inquiry were simply left waiting when the laboratory closed.

Wumpus Labs is a small monument to those days (and to the people who made them worth remembering).

01 / SIM-INT

Simulated Intelligence, tight coding, meta-programming, and the occasional encounter with a very large cave.

02 / CODE

Experiments in programming languages, engine hacking, and building things that probably work or at least once did.

03 / LABS

Projects, experiments, teaching materials, and assorted digital artifacts from the laboratory.
